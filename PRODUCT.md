# BidSort: product brief

**What it is:** an idea-validation site for BidSort, an app that gathers new public construction bids from North Carolina's state, county, city and school bid sites and keeps only the ones that match a subcontractor's trade and area, with a daily alert.

**Who it's for:** small NC subcontractors (concrete, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, sitework, painting, masonry) who bid public jobs. Most of them look for bids at night after work.

**The problem (from 5 phone interviews, Oct 2026):**
- 3 of 5 subs check several bid sites every week and have already tried to fix it.
- Darnell spends 4–5 hours a week, and Angela about 3.
- Angela's email alerts send too many jobs that aren't in her trade, so the real problem is sorting by trade, not just finding bids.

**This site's job:** let a visitor understand the idea in 10 seconds, watch a 60–90 s video, try the main flow (trade → feed → alert), then answer 3 questions about whether they'd use it and pay for it.

**One primary action:** Try the Demo. **Second action:** the interest form.

**What we're measuring:** form answers. Suggested go signal: at least 10 answers from real subs, with at least 30% choosing "pay" or "use if free".

**Not a live product.** The site says so, and the demo is labelled as sample data.
